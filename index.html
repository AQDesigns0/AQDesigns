07 / Good to know

Questions,

What can AQ Designs customise? +

Almost anything wearable: hoodies, T-shirts, jackets, school uniforms, caps and more. Tell us what you have in mind and we’ll help find the best approach.

Do you deliver outside Pretoria? +

Yes. We are based in Pretoria and can arrange delivery anywhere in South Africa.

What is the minimum wholesale order? +

Our minimum wholesale order is 15 items. Get in touch for available styles, sizes and pricing.

How do I get a quote? +

Send us a WhatsApp with your idea, quantity and deadline. We’ll take it from there.
